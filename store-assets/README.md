Screenshots were captured November 6, 2026 in a 1280 by 800 iframe through the managed browser preview. The outside preview margins were removed and JPEG captures were converted to PNG. Text and highlighted content were not composited or rewritten. These captures demonstrate the extension UI and runtime in the browser; they are not evidence of native Chrome installation or permission-prompt testing. Sample text is synthetic.

These images do not show or claim eye tracking inside the extension. The optional webcam experiment is separate.

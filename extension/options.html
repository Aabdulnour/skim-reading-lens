What stays on your device?

Reading goal, dock size, pause state, and the sites you chose to remember. Skim does not store a browsing history or upload page text. A quiet-reader import is kept temporarily in Chrome’s session storage until the reader consumes it, the next import clears it, or the browser session ends.

Text already open in a reader stays in that tab’s memory until you close or reload it.

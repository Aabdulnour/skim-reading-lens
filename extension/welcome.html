The short version is in here somewhere.

Reading online is often a treasure hunt. There is an answer in here, but it is surrounded by introductions, extra context, and another explanation of the explanation. Skim helps a useful sentence stand out while the full paragraph stays available.

You do not need another dashboard or a complicated routine. Start with one website you already read every day, then choose “Remember on this site” from the Skim popup. Chrome asks for access to that site before the lens can start automatically.

The words stay exactly as they were written. However, a highlighted sentence can still be wrong, incomplete, or missing an important qualification nearby. Use the lens to find your place, then check the surrounding text and its sources.

You can switch the dock between the answer, next steps, the catch, and key details. There are 4 reading goals, and changing one updates the local sentence selection. Nothing here measures how much you understood or claims to read your mind.

Try “The catch” in the dock, then hover over the third paragraph.
◉

Your reading stays yours.

Skim reads the paragraph under your pointer on your device. It does not upload page text, record your camera, or store browsing history. Reading preferences and remembered sites stay in your Chrome profile. Text you explicitly open in the quiet reader is transferred through temporary browser session storage.
